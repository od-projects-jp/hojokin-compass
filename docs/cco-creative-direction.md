# CCO creative direction — 2026-10

## Core decision
Stop treating the site as an administrative database UI. Build a discoverable, welcoming consumer service with a trustworthy professional business workspace. Keep one brand identity across both.

## Visual language
- Consumer: warm pastel blocks, large meaningful illustrations and pictograms, approachable Japanese copy, clear category and location pathways.
- Business: confident green and neutral blue, restrained accents, prominent search, legible filters and primary actions.
- Brand: deep forest green, cream background, gentle secondary colors, rounded cards. No imitation of municipal site artwork or assets.
- Original inline SVG graphics only. No third-party scripts, cookies, accounts, tracking or personal data collection.
- Primary CTAs use action-oriented language: 「暮らしの支援を探す」「事業の補助金を探す」「公式情報を見る」.
- Accessible semantic anchors/buttons, focus indicators, responsive mobile layouts, readable contrast.

## Experience principles
1. Show a clear benefit and action in the first screen.
2. Make the category tiles navigable, not merely decorative. Homepage topic links pass a validated query parameter to the individual search.
3. Show area and eligibility before directing to official sources.
4. Do not imply all Kanto municipalities are covered.
5. Keep category labels, illustration treatment and CTAs consistent across the site.

## Follow-up quality bar
Review at 375px, 768px, 1440px viewport; keyboard navigation; deep-link behavior; contrast; actual result quality. Compare search activation, organic landing-page performance and official-source CTA engagement only with privacy-reviewed measurement.
